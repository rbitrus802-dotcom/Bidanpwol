Who We Are

FORGED FROM
THE EARTH UP

Bidanpwol Global Mining Limited is a leading Nigerian mining company with deep roots in the Plateau State region. We combine decades of geological expertise with modern extraction technology to responsibly develop Nigeria's vast mineral wealth — creating lasting value for investors, communities, and the nation.

🌍
Our Mission
To responsibly extract and process Nigeria's mineral resources, generating sustainable wealth while preserving our environment and uplifting host communities.
🔭
Our Vision
To become West Africa's most trusted and technologically advanced mining group by 2030, known for operational excellence and ethical practice.
🤝
Our Values
Safety first. Environmental stewardship. Community partnership. Transparency in all dealings. These are non-negotiable at every level of our operations.

Resources Portfolio

MINERALS WE MINE
& PROCESS

Nigeria's geology is extraordinarily rich. We operate across multiple mineral categories, positioning us as a diversified, resilient mining enterprise.

Sn
Tin Ore (Cassiterite)
Our flagship mineral — the Plateau State hosts one of Africa's richest tin belts, and we operate multiple high-yield cassiterite sites.
Cb
Columbite-Tantalite
A critical high-value rare metal essential for electronics and aerospace manufacturing. We are a major supplier to global markets.
Au
Gold
Artisanal and industrial gold recovery operations with full chain-of-custody documentation for responsible sourcing certification.
Zn
Zinc & Lead
Galena and sphalerite extraction from established north-central Nigeria mineral belts, processed to international grade specifications.
Fe
Iron Ore
High-grade iron ore deposits targeted for Nigeria's growing domestic steel industry and export to regional partners.
Li
Lithium
Emerging opportunity — Nigeria's identified lithium deposits are being fast-tracked for the global clean energy transition market.
Mn
Manganese
Industrial-grade manganese processing for steel alloy applications and battery technology demand from international clients.
Qz
Quartz / Silica Sand
High-purity silica extraction for glass, ceramics, and semiconductor-grade applications meeting international purity thresholds.
